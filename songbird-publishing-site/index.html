About us

Small enough to know your manuscript by name.

"We're not interested in the safest version of your book. We're interested in the true one."

Song Bird Publishing was founded by editors and designers who were tired of watching good books get flattened by slow, impersonal presses. We work with a small roster of authors at a time — never a slush pile you disappear into.

From first-draft feedback to a finished, distributed book, you keep creative control and the rights to your work. We bring the craft; you bring the voice.

  • Editorial first. Every project starts with a developmental read, not a sales pitch.
  • Design that fits the story. Covers and layouts built for your genre and your reader, not a template.
  • You keep your rights. Song Bird is a service, not a gatekeeper — your book stays yours.
  • Honest timelines. We tell you what's realistic before you sign anything.
